# 🏗️ Construction Cost Calculator for Indian Homes

A free, easy-to-use **construction planning and cost estimation tool** designed specifically for Indian homeowners, builders, and professionals. This tool helps estimate **construction, renovation, and material costs** including flooring, painting, plumbing, electrical, and more.

🔗 **Live Website**: [toolwebsite.in](https://toolwebsite.in/)

---

## 📋 Features

- 🏠 **House Construction Calculator** – Estimate total construction costs  
- ⚒️ **Material & Labour Calculator** – Budget for raw materials and labor  
- 🧱 **Floor & Wall Tile Calculator** – Estimate flooring and tiling expenses  
- 🎨 **Paint Calculator** – Calculate paint requirements & costs  
- 🚰 **Plumbing Estimator** – Plan plumbing expenses for your house  
- ⚡ **Electrical Estimator** – Estimate electrical layout costs  
- 🚪 **Doors & Windows Calculator** – Cost estimation for fittings  
- 🖌️ **Paint Visualization Tool** – Try different wall color options
