Our Policy

At Tool Website Hub, we are committed to delivering accurate and reliable construction planning information. Our suite of tools is developed using current market data and is regularly updated to reflect the evolving trends in construction costs across India. While our calculators provide detailed estimates for various aspects of your project—such as house construction, material and labour costs, tiling, paint, plumbing, electrical work, and more— please note that actual expenses may vary due to factors including location, specific material choices, and local labour rates. We recommend using our tools as a starting point and consulting with local professionals for final budgeting and planning.

FAQ

How accurate are the cost estimates provided by your tools?

Our tools use up-to-date market data to provide average cost estimates for various construction components. However, final costs may differ due to local factors such as material quality, labour rates, and project-specific requirements.

Which planning tools are available on Tool Website?

We offer a comprehensive range of tools including house construction and material & labour estimators, floor & wall tile calculators, paint calculators and visualization, plumbing and electrical cost estimators, doors & windows calculators, floor plan creator, and civil engineering estimators.

How frequently is your cost data updated?

We continuously update our data to ensure our estimates reflect current market trends. Still, we advise verifying with local contractors for the most accurate pricing.

Are the planning tools free to use?

Yes, all of our planning tools are free to use. We are dedicated to providing accessible resources to help you plan your construction project effectively.
