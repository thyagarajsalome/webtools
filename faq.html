Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tool Website Hub?

Tool Website Hub is your comprehensive resource for construction planning in India. We offer a suite of free planning tools for homeowners, builders, and professionals.

What kind of tools do you provide?

Our calculators and estimators cover various aspects including house construction costs, budgeting for materials and labour, floor & wall tiling, paint calculations, plumbing, electrical planning, doors & windows cost, and even civil engineering estimations.

Are these tools free to use?

Yes, all of our tools are completely free and are continuously updated to reflect the latest market trends.

How accurate are the cost estimations?

Our tools provide accurate cost estimates based on up-to-date market trends and location-specific pricing. However, for detailed construction planning, consulting a professional is recommended.

Do I need to register to use the website?

No registration is required. You can access our planning tools immediately on the website without creating an account.

How often are the tools updated?

We update our tools regularly to ensure that you have access to the latest market data and trends.
