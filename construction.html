How to Use & Benefits

Welcome to our House Construction Budget Calculator! This tool is designed to offer you a clear estimation of your construction cost in India by breaking down the total budget into key components such as Foundation, Structure, Roofing, Finishing, and Interiors.

Simply input the total built-up area (in sq.ft) and the cost per square foot in INR, and the calculator will do the rest. It provides both a summarized cost breakdown and a detailed component analysis, ensuring you understand where each rupee is allocated.

The benefits of using this tool include streamlined budgeting, enhanced transparency in cost distribution, and the ability to share or reset the budget easily. Whether you're planning a new home or managing an existing project, this calculator is here to help you make informed financial decisions.
