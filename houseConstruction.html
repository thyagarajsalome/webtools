Why Use Our House Construction Estimator?

Estimating construction costs can be overwhelming — that’s why we built this free tool to help homeowners, builders, and developers get a quick idea of potential expenses. Whether you're building a new house or adding more floors, this tool offers clarity with just a few inputs.

How to Use the Tool

Simply select your construction type (e.g., house, villa), enter the built-up area in square feet, choose the quality level, and specify the number of floors. The tool will instantly calculate the estimated cost, with separate breakdowns for material + labour and labour-only contracts.

  • ✅ Helps compare different quality levels and project scales
  • ✅ Ideal for budgeting before starting your construction
